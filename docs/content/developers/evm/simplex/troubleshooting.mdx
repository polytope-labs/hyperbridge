# Troubleshooting

For verbose output, set `logging = "debug"` in `[simplex]`, or pick a level on the dashboard's **Logs** page. Report bugs to the Hyperbridge GitHub with full logs (redact private keys).

## **Nothing is being filled**

Check the **Limit orders** list on the Overview page. Simplex fills only against open limit orders, so a solver with none posted skips every order. If orders are open, look in the log for `No limit order matches this order`. Its `reason` names the check the closest order failed:

| Reason | What to change |
|---|---|
| no limit order is open | Post a [limit order](/developers/evm/simplex/limit-orders). |
| none fills on the order's destination chain | Post an order that fills on that chain. |
| none takes the order's input token in / none pays out the token the order asks for | You have no order in that direction. Remember that each order is one-way. |
| none accepts swaps from the order's source chain | Post a new order that includes that chain in **Accepts swaps from**. |
| it asks for X; the best limit order offers Y | Your rate is worse than the swapper wants. |
| the orders that meet its rate have nothing left to pay out | Your orders at that rate are used up. Post more depth. |

---

## **No profitable strategy found**

The order matched, but it is worth less than [`minOrderSizeUsd`](/developers/evm/simplex/configuration#simplex) (default $20) and `order.fees` do not cover gas plus, for cross-chain orders, the relayer fee. Or a same-asset transfer would not net a positive spread. See [How orders are matched and filled](/developers/evm/simplex/limit-orders#how-orders-are-matched-and-filled). This is normal for orders with low fees.

---

## **An order is skipped before it is priced**

`Skipping order: its deadline has passed on the destination chain` means the order cannot be filled by anyone. An order's deadline is a block number on its destination chain, set when the order is built. An order that takes too long to land on its source chain arrives already expired. The log carries the `deadline` and the destination `head` it was compared with.

`Error estimating gas, not bidding on this order` means the bundler could not simulate the fill, so the fill would revert. Simplex sends no bid without the bundler's estimate. The log carries the bundler's error. If it appears on every order for a chain, check that chain's bundler URL.

---

## **A limit order is refused when posting**

The form shows the reason. The common ones are:

- **The wallet cannot pay it out**, for example `The wallet holds 1000 in the wallet and 200 in vaults USDC on EVM-8453, which cannot pay out 1500`. Fund the fill chain, or post a smaller amount.
- **Below the orderbook's dust floor.** The amount paid out is under the orderbook's minimum for that token.
- **The pair, token or chain is not served.** The orderbook does not list that token on the fill chain or on one of the accepted source chains.

---

## **A limit order is amber, or shows `UNDER_FUNDED`**

An amber line under a live order means the order is open, but the last posting or check reported a problem. The line shows the reason, and opening the order shows it in full. A failed posting is retried on the next reconciliation. `UNDER_FUNDED` means your balance on the fill chain no longer covers the order, so the orderbook advertises less than its size. Top up the chain or cancel the order.

---

## **`Orderbook at <url> returned HTTP 405`, or other orderbook errors**

Check `[orderbook] url`. It should be the HyperFX GraphQL endpoint for your network, such as `https://orderbook.hyperfx.finance/mainnet/graphql`. While the orderbook is unreachable, Simplex keeps filling against its local copy of your limit orders, but cannot post new ones.

---

## **Insufficient token balances**

Deposit more of the token your limit orders pay out on their fill chains, or let your [treasury vaults](/developers/evm/simplex/treasury) hold it.

---

## **RPC rate limiting (429 errors)**

Switch to a premium RPC provider, reduce `maxConcurrentOrders`, or raise `blockScanIntervalSeconds`.

---

## **`Hyperbridge runs no bundler for <chain>, so simplex cannot fill there`**

Simplex refuses to start, or to add or switch a chain to filling, when Hyperbridge runs no [bundler](/developers/evm/simplex/configuration#bundler) for it, since every fill is a UserOperation a bundler submits. Mark the chain watch-only, or remove it.

---

## **`Bundler <host> for <chain> does not support EntryPoint <address>`**

Simplex refuses to start, or to add or switch a chain, when a chain's bundler answers `eth_supportedEntryPoints` without the EntryPoint that chain's solver account uses, since every fill UserOperation would be rejected. That points at a fault in Hyperbridge's bundler for the chain; mark the chain watch-only until it is fixed. A bundler that does not answer, or whose answer cannot be read, is only logged as a warning.

---

## **Bid submission fails**

- Confirm that `substratePrivateKey` and `hyperbridgeWsUrl` are set, and that your Substrate account holds BRIDGE tokens.
- On chains with a paymaster (Circle or Simplex), make sure the solver's smart account holds at least 1 USDC (or 1 USDT for the `SimplexPaymaster`), so the paymaster can cover gas.
- On chains without a paymaster, the solver needs a native balance. Simplex automatically tops up the ERC-4337 EntryPoint deposit to cover `targetGasUnits` (3M by default) at the current gas price.
- If you use MPC Vault, verify that the client-signer container is running and reachable.

---

## **Vault withdrawal failed**

A fill withdraws any shortfall from your ERC-4626 vaults in the same transaction. Check that the vault holds enough of the underlying token, and that it is not paused or capping withdrawals. The log includes the revert reason.
