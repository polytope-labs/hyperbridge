# 2026-09-22 — Intent quotes, liquidity and rates read the HyperFX orderbook

`IntentGateway.quoteIntent`, `queryAvailableLiquidity` and `queryBuyAndSellRates` read the HyperFX orderbook's GraphQL API (`ORDERBOOK_URLS`: `https://orderbook.hyperfx.finance/testnet/graphql` for a testnet destination chain, `…/mainnet/graphql` otherwise, via `orderbookUrlFor`; overridable with `withOrderbook(url | HyperFxOrderbook)`). They no longer need `withQueryClient`. The orderbook is their only source: the `indexed_rates`, `phantom_snapshot` and `uniswap_v4` quote strategies are gone, along with the `strategy` and `uniswapV4` params.

- `HyperFxOrderbook` (`src/protocols/intents/orderbook/client.ts`) sends three documents: `topOfBook` (`books` plus `bestRate` in both directions), `routeLiquidity` (with `books`, to name the side) and `quote`. Amounts and rates stay 1e18 `bigint`s, and rates are quote per 1 base. `OrderbookMarket` (`market.ts`) resolves configured token addresses and symbols to orderbook symbols and state machine ids, and converts raw amounts to and from 1e18.
- **Exact input** is the orderbook's `quote`: a clearing price that may combine several solvers' orders. **Exact output** starts from `routeLiquidity.bestRate` and re-quotes at each clearing price until the quote delivers the output, for at most 16 rounds. The input is rounded up to a whole raw unit, and nothing past `maxFillableIn` is quoted. A route that cannot fill throws `InsufficientOrderbookLiquidityError`, carrying `maxFillableIn` in raw source-token units; running out of rounds throws `OrderbookQuoteNotConvergedError` instead.
- Orderbook prices already carry the gateway protocol-fee haircut, so the SDK no longer reads `protocolFeeBps` or adjusts amounts for it.
- `AvailableLiquidity` now reports `route`, `side`, `bestRate`, true `availableLiquidity`, virtual `depthIn`/`depthOut`, `maxFillableIn` and counts. `BuyAndSellRates` now reports `bid`, `ask`, `mid`, `spread` and `spreadBps` in the book's quote-per-base units. `bid` and `ask` count orders filled on the destination that accept the source chain.
- Phantom-order quoting is removed. That covers `aggregatePhantomBids` and its weighting, balance and Uniswap V4 position reads, and the coprocessor's phantom-order polling and phantom-bid submission. The declaration codec and `fetchBidsForOrder`/`setAggregationFetch` move to `phantom-bid.ts`: simplex signs orderbook posts with the codec, and the indexer enriches bids with `fetchBidsForOrder`.
- Indexer-backed pricing is removed: `LiquidityEngine`, the `AVAILABLE_LIQUIDITY`, `BUY_AND_SELL_RATES` and phantom-snapshot queries, and the Uniswap V4 pool list in chain config.
- BSC Chapel (`0x2bbbd701…778a`) and Polygon Amoy (`0xe4ff5d2a…a758`) now configure cNGN, the hCNGN testnet stand-in listed in the indexer's `config-testnet.json`, with 6 decimals.
- Simplex defaults to the same per-network URLs (`DEFAULT_ORDERBOOK_URLS`). Its setup wizard writes the one for the selected network, and on an update run replaces a built-in or retired default (`RETIRED_ORDERBOOK_URLS`) while keeping an operator's own URL.
- `quoteUniswap`, `UniswapQuoteEngine` and `Swap` stay. They convert gas to fee tokens and value bids; they are not intent quotes.

Validation: `orderbookSchema.test.ts` validates the SDK's documents and the docs page's GraphQL examples against simplex's pinned orderbook schema, refreshed here from `hyperfx-orderbook` main. The `orderbook schema pin` workflow is removed; the pin is refreshed by hand. `orderbookMarket.test.ts` covers scaling, exact output, orientation, spreads and errors against a stubbed transport. The sequential `IntentGateway orderbook reads` suite runs on BSC Chapel and Polygon Amoy against the testnet deployment.
